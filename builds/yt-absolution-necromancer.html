Absolution Necromancer

Absolution é um feitiço que acerta a área e invoca sentinelas a partir dos inimigos mortos. O plano é começar a liga com Absolution e, com mais currency, trocar para o unique Chains of Command.

Ascensão

  1. 1º lab
    Commander of Darkness

    Auras from your Skills grant 3% increased Attack and Cast Speed to you and Allies / You and nearby Allies deal 30% increased Damage / You and nearby Allies have +30% to Elemental Resistances

  2. 2º lab
    Mindless Aggression

    Minions have 20% more Maximum Life / Minions deal 10% more Damage

  3. 3º lab
    Unnatural Strength

    Minions have Unholy Might

  4. 4º lab
    Bone Barrier

    1% additional Physical Damage Reduction per Minion, up to 10% / 1% of Damage Dealt by your Minions is Leeched to you as Life / Minions gain 40% of Maximum Life as Extra Maximum Energy Shield
